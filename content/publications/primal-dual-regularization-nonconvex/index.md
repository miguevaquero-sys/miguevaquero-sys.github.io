# Abstract source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19916
abstract: |-
  We study continuous-time primal-dual dynamics for tracking solutions of time-dependent constrained
  optimization problems. First, for uniformly strongly convex-strongly concave saddle functions, we
  establish explicit finite-time tracking and additive-disturbance bounds. For convex problems, we
  then introduce a directional primal regularization, combined with dual Tikhonov regularization,
  and quantify the resulting optimization bias and constraint violation. For smooth nonconvex
  problems, we couple a proximal local convexification with a two-time-scale primal-dual flow and
  obtain a local tracking result under constraint qualification, second-order sufficiency, and
  temporal regularity. Numerical traffic-signal experiments at a single intersection evaluate the
  proposed method under vehicle-throughput and pedestrian-aware objectives, comparing it with
  standard signal-control schemes.
