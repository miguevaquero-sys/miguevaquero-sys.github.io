# Abstract source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1209.4003
abstract: |-
  In this paper we introduce poly-Poisson structures as a higher-order extension of Poisson
  structures. It is shown that any poly-Poisson structure is endowed with a polysymplectic foliation.
  It is also proved that if a Lie group acts polysymplectically on a polysymplectic manifold then,
  under certain regularity conditions, the reduced space is a poly-Poisson manifold. In addition, some
  interesting examples of poly-Poisson manifolds are discussed.
